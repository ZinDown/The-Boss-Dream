We recruited testers by inviting volunteers from personal contacts and relevant online communities. 
Participants opted in using the official Google Play closed testing link and tested the app on their own devices. 
The goal was to collect real-world feedback on stability, performance, and user experience.


2

Testers actively engaged with the app throughout the closed testing period. 
They used the app regularly to check data updates, interact with the Live2D UI, and explore historical records. 
Feedback was provided through direct messages and informal reports, helping us identify minor UI issues, performance improvements, and stability-related fixes.


3
We received constructive feedback from testers during the closed testing period. 
Feedback was collected through direct messages and informal reports after testers used the app on their own devices. 
Testers reported suggestions related to UI clarity, Live2D animation smoothness, data loading speed, and minor usability improvements. 
Based on this feedback, we optimized performance, refined UI interactions, and fixed minor stability issues.
